## Subscription refunds
- Upgrades are charged the full new-plan price immediately, with no proration. We do not refund the old plan's unused time.
- Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. Unused time is not refunded.
- **Exception:** if a customer upgraded by mistake and has used fewer than 100 credits since the upgrade, Tier 2 may refund the upgrade within 7 days. One exception per team.
- Annual plans: no refunds after 30 days from purchase.
## Pay-as-you-go top-ups
Pay-as-you-go charges are not refunded, because the credits are added to the account at once. If a customer set no monthly pay-as-you-go limit and was surprised by the charges, explain how to set the limit in billing settings. Tier 2 may refund the most recent 5 USD increments, up to 50 USD in total, once per team.
## Goodwill credits
Use goodwill credits when Firecrawl caused the problem (an incident, a confirmed bug), not when the customer misconfigured a job.

| Situation | Credit |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed bug that wasted the customer's credits | the credits wasted, up to the tier limit |
| Sev 1 incident affecting the customer | 10% of their monthly plan credits |
| Missed first-response target (paid plans) | 500 credits |

Tier 1 can grant up to 500 credits per ticket. Tier 2 can grant up to 5,000 credits per ticket. Anything above 5,000 needs the support lead.
Goodwill credits are bonus credits. Like credit coupons, they stay on the account if the customer changes plan.
## Never refund
- Credits used on pages that returned 403 or 404 (the document was returned, so the charge is correct).
- Credits used by a leaked API key. Help the customer rotate the key and set a per-key spend limit instead.
