Support does not quote prices or negotiate. Hand these requests to sales with the ticket link and the team id.
## Hand off to sales when the customer asks for
- More than the Scale plan offers: more than 1,000,000 monthly credits or more than 100 concurrent browsers.
- SSO, a custom SLA, a Data Processing Agreement, or invoicing by purchase order.
- Zero data retention for search, or Key Restrictions (both are enabled per organization).
- Custom rate limits or custom concurrency.
- A discount, a custom quote, or a multi-year contract.
## Do not hand off
- Upgrades between self-serve plans (Hobby, Standard, Growth, Scale). Point the customer to Change Plan in billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately.
- Monthly to yearly switches. Monthly to yearly at the same or a higher credit tier is an immediate upgrade the customer can do themselves.
- Coupon questions. Credit coupons are redeemed in the Billing section; subscription coupons only at Stripe checkout.
## How to hand off
1. Tag the ticket `sales-handoff`.
2. Post the ticket link, team id, current plan and the request in one sentence in the sales hand-off channel.
3. Reply to the customer: sales will contact them within 1 business day. Do not promise a price, a feature date or a discount.
## Startups and nonprofits
We do not have a published startup or nonprofit discount. If a customer asks, hand off to sales and say no discount is guaranteed.
