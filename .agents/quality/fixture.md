# Fictional support source: Harbor Notes

This document is invented solely for controlled evaluation. It is not a real product policy and must not be presented as demo evidence.

## Plans and exports

Harbor Notes has a Free plan and a Team plan. The Free plan permits up to 3 notebooks. The Team plan permits up to 50 notebooks per workspace. Both plans can export a notebook as Markdown. Only the Team plan can export a workspace as a ZIP archive.

## Deletion and recovery

When a notebook is deleted, it stays in the workspace trash for 30 days. A workspace owner can restore it during that period. After 30 days it is permanently deleted. Deleting a workspace permanently deletes its notebooks immediately; the notebook trash recovery period does not apply to workspace deletion.

## Billing and refunds

An owner may cancel a Team subscription from Settings > Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Support agents cannot promise refunds; refund requests must be reviewed by the billing team. This document does not state a refund eligibility policy.

## Access and incidents

Workspace owners can invite members by email from Settings > Members. A person must accept an invitation before joining. For a suspected account compromise, advise the customer to reset their password and escalate the incident to the security team. This source does not give the security team's response time.

## Conflicting sync information

The setup guide says offline changes sync automatically when the device reconnects. A later troubleshooting note says automatic sync after reconnection is not currently supported. Neither passage provides a date or identifies itself as authoritative. Treat these as unresolved conflicting claims.
